I am a recent B.Sc. graduate in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, currently preparing for graduate study in Computer Science, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence.

My background in ETE gave me a strong foundation in signals, systems, and applied mathematics, which I have since carried into applied machine learning work: natural language processing, AI-based classification systems, and automation pipelines built around large language models. My undergraduate capstone project, [Scam Detector](/portfolio/), used speech-to-text transcription and an LLM-based classifier to detect phone scams in real time, alerting a trusted contact automatically when a call was flagged.

I currently work as a Technical Lead / AI Automation Engineer at CRMCrew Ltd. (UK-based, remote), where I build AI agent workflows, custom API integrations, and Zapier/n8n automations for clients.
