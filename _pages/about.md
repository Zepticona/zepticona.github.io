I'm a B.Sc. graduate in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, working on applied machine learning across NLP, agentic AI, time series forecasting, and network intrusion detection, and preparing to apply to Ph.D. programs.

My current independent research investigates why network traffic forecasting collapses at fine-grained IP level, developing cross-level information sharing methods that combine foundation models with forecast reconciliation on the CESNET-TimeSeries24 dataset. My undergraduate thesis designed an all-dielectric metasurface sensor for near-infrared refractive index sensing, and my capstone project, [Scam Detector](/portfolio/), used speech-to-text transcription and an LLM classifier to flag phone scams in real time.

I currently work as Technical Lead (AI Automation Engineer) at CRMCrew Ltd. (UK, remote), designing autonomous AI agents for end-to-end business workflow automation.
